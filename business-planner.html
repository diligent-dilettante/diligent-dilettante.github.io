why i built a business planner that argues with you

august 2026 · sri krishna ravavarapu · code on github

tl;dr: a free claude skill that stress-tests your business idea instead of flattering it. get it here.

ask an AI about your business idea and it will say the idea is promising. it says this to everyone.

a good advisor does the opposite. the best one i had killed a bad idea of mine in ten minutes. that saved me six months.

i ran a foundry in vizag for three and a half years. factories don't die of bad narratives. they die of thin margins and slow-paying customers. the ninety days between paying your supplier and getting paid yourself will sink you while the pitch deck still looks great. i built the planner around that.

how it works

four questions before anything else. does anyone want this. can one unit make money. can you reach the buyer. is there a rule that ends it on day one. most ideas should fail here. failing here is cheap. failing later is not.

then it interviews you, section by section. unit economics can't be skipped. every number is tagged: sourced, founder-supplied, or estimate. override a warning and it writes down your reason and moves on. correct a fact and it takes your word. you know your market. it doesn't.

it also knows most businesses shouldn't be startups. a shop clearing a lakh a month is a good business. and when a test is cheap, it says go test.

every conversation ends in a decision: go, no-go, pivot, or pilot.

try it

free and open source: github.com/diligent-dilettante/business-planner. if it saves you from a bad bet, write to me.
