why i built a business planner that argues with you

9 August 2026 · 3 min read · Sri Krishna Ravavarapu · code on GitHub

tl;dr: business-planner is a free, open-source bundle of six Claude skills that interrogates your business idea and ends in a go, no-go, pivot or pilot decision. built for physical and offline businesses. MIT licensed. get it here.

ask an AI about your business idea and it will tell you the idea is promising. it says this to everyone.

the best advisor i ever had killed an idea of mine in ten minutes.

i ran a foundry in Vizag for three and a half years. the thing that actually hurt was the ninety days between paying a supplier and getting paid ourselves. the pitch deck looked fine that entire time. i built the planner around that gap.

what business planner is

business-planner is a bundle of six Claude skills for building and growing physical or offline businesses. a controller skill runs an interview-led plan, and pulls in five sub-skills as the plan needs them. it is free, MIT licensed, and open source.

business-plannerthe controller. interviews you section by section, applies named frameworks, checks biases, locks each section only when it holds.
founder-fitprofiles whoever is actually running this. maps each part of the plan to run it yourself, de-risk it, or get help.
market-simrehearses the plan against a synthetic customer panel. B2C, B2B buying committees, B2G tenders, B2B2C channels.
competition-sima wargame with the customer as referee. you correct the competitor profiles before anything is simulated.
strategy-opsturns the surviving plan into a strategy doc, a 90-day operating plan, and an investment card.
whispera quiet advisor watching the whole flow for precedent and bias. silence is its default.

how it works

four questions before anything else. does anyone want this. can one unit make money. can you reach the buyer. is there a rule that ends it on day one. an idea that dies here cost you ten minutes.

then it interviews you, section by section. unit economics can't be skipped. every number is tagged: sourced, founder-supplied, or estimate. override a warning and it writes down your reason and moves on. correct a fact and it takes your word. you know your market. it doesn't.

once the plan holds together it gets rehearsed. a synthetic customer panel raises objections and prices. a competitor wargame plays your moves against reactions you set the triggers for. simulation sits at the bottom of the evidence ladder, under stated interest, interviews, smoke tests and pre-orders, and the skill says so every time it runs one.

it also knows most businesses shouldn't be startups. a shop clearing a lakh a month is a good business. when a test is cheap, it says go test.

every engagement ends in a decision: go, no-go, pivot, or pilot. plus the single riskiest assumption and one cheap dated next step.

how to install business planner

in Claude Code:

/plugin marketplace add diligent-dilettante/business-planner
/plugin install business-planner

in Claude Desktop or Claude.ai, upload the skill folders under settings, capabilities, skills. or add them to a project's knowledge. then just describe what you're doing: "i want to start a furniture brand selling online, is it worth it", or "my bakery does well locally, should i add a second product line". the controller picks the track and pulls in the sub-skills.

questions

who is it for?

physical and offline businesses at any scale. solo operators, shops, MSMEs, D2C brands, retail, food, services, manufacturing, funded startups. it calibrates to three ambition levels, livelihood, growth and venture, and treats a solo operator clearing a good living as a real success.

how is it different from asking ChatGPT for a business plan?

a plain chat produces an optimistic plan for any idea you give it. this one runs the kill screen first, makes unit economics non-skippable, tags the provenance of every number, and refuses to end without a decision.

is it free?

yes. MIT licensed, open source, no account or key beyond your existing Claude access.

does it work outside India?

yes. the method is country-neutral. the India reference files, GST, Udyam, MSME capital sources, city-tier segmentation, load only when the plan is india-based.

what does it produce?

a plan document, a strategy doc with scenarios and tripwires, a 90-day operating plan, and an investment card showing money and time in, payback, NPV at your real discount rate, IRR by scenario, drawdown and reversibility. the card is what lets you compare two unrelated ideas.
